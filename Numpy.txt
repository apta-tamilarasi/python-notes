
ADVANCED NUMPY – 20 TOPICS
EACH TOPIC WITH 5 EXAMPLES (INPUT & OUTPUT)
==========================================

Assume:
a = np.array([1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6])
b = np.array([10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60])
A = np.array([[1, 2], [3, 4]])
B = np.array([[5, 6], [7, 8]])

------------------------------------------
TOPIC 1: Broadcasting
1) Input: a + 5
   Output: [6 7 8 9 10 11]

2) Input: a + np.array([1])
   Output: [2 3 4 5 6 7]

3) Input: np.ones((2,3)) + [1,2,3]
   Output: [[2 3 4],[2 3 4]]

4) Input: np.arange(3) + np.arange(3).reshape(3,1)
   Output: [[0 1 2],[1 2 3],[2 3 4]]

5) Input: np.zeros((3,1)) + [5,6,7]
   Output: [[5 6 7],[5 6 7],[5 6 7]]
